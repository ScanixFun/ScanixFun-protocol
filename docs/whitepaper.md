# Scanix Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets move faster than anyone can follow by hand. Scanix exists to make the busiest corner of Solana legible at a glance: one board, ranked by what actually traded, with nothing standing between the reader and the numbers.

The mission is simple. Show where volume is gathering, show it plainly, and keep it honest by displaying figures exactly as the source reports them.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: The board

A ranked, self-refreshing board of the ten busiest Solana pairs, with a ticker, tabs and one-tap address copy.

### Phase 2: Depth of read

More ways to slice the same data: longer windows, filters by depth, and side-by-side pair views.

### Phase 3: Alerts

Optional notices when a pair crosses a volume or momentum threshold that the reader sets.

### Phase 4: Open tooling

Public documentation and a small open client so others can build on the same reading of the market.
